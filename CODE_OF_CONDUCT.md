# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on technical content, not personal attacks.
- Accept feedback gracefully.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may remove comments, commits, issues, or contributions that violate this code.
