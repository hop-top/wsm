## LinkedIn Article

**Title: Your Coding History Doesn't Belong to Your IDE**

Every AI coding assistant you use keeps a history. Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, Copilot, Gemini—each one remembers what you worked on.

But none of them share those memories with each other. And none of them give those memories to you.

**What Your History Contains:**

Think about what's recorded in your AI coding sessions: requirements discussions, architectural decisions, code exploration, debugging sessions, dead ends explored, trade-offs considered.

This isn't just chat logs. This is your professional memory. It's worth more than the code itself.

**The Problem:**

Your history lives on vendor servers you don't control. You can't export it. Can't query it. Can't use it in another tool. Can't back it up on your own terms.

Switching tools means abandoning months of context.

**What Ownership Looks Like:**

Local storage. Open format. Full export. Cross-tool access. Vendor independence.

WSM stores your history in a local SQLite database. You own the file. You control the format.
