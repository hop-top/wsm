## LinkedIn Article

**Title: Why Your IDE Shouldn't Own Your History**

Your coding history is the most valuable artifact you produce. It contains every decision, every exploration, every dead end, every breakthrough.

And you're giving it away to vendors who don't share it back.

**The Current State:**

Every AI coding tool stores your session history. But you can't export it. You can't query it. You can't use it in another tool. It's locked in a proprietary vault you'll never see.

- Claude Code → Anthropic's infrastructure
- Cursor → Cursor's infrastructure
- Copilot → Microsoft's infrastructure

**The Real Cost:**

Your session history contains requirements discussions, architecture decisions, debugging sessions, dead ends explored, trade-offs considered.

This is institutional memory. It's worth more than the code itself. Code tells you what was built. History tells you why.

When history is scattered across vendors, you end up with five partial memories instead of one complete one.

**What Ownership Looks Like:**

Local storage. Open format. Full export. Cross-tool visibility. Your data, your control.

WSM stores history in a local SQLite database. You own the file. You control the format.
