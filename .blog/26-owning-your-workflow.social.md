## LinkedIn Article

**Title: WSM Standalone: Owning Your Workflow History**

We've covered a lot of ground. Let's bring it together.

**What We Built:**

1. **History Ownership** — Your coding history doesn't belong to your IDE. It belongs to you.

2. **Unified Import** — One history from many tools. Format adapters translate everything.

3. **Session Branching** — Git for conversations. Fork, explore, merge, preserve.

4. **Audit Trail** — The memory you didn't know you needed. Every question answerable.

5. **Cross-Machine Continuity** — Your session, everywhere. Without the cloud.

**What You Get:**

- Ownership: Your history in a file you control
- Portability: Export, move, back up anytime
- Unification: One history from many tools
- Exploration: Branch, merge, preserve alternatives
- Accountability: Complete audit trail
- Continuity: Work across machines seamlessly
- Privacy: No cloud required, no vendor dependency
- Query: CLI, SQL, JSON—pick your interface

**The Philosophy:**

Your data belongs to you. History is valuable. Tools should serve you. Privacy is a right. Simplicity wins.

Your coding history is your professional memory. Treat it like the asset it is.
